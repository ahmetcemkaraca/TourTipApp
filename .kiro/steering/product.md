# Product Overview

TourTrip.app is a comprehensive mobile super application designed to connect tourists, locals, and activity seekers with service providers offering tours, trips, events, and other activities in City X. The platform serves as a one-stop solution for discovering, comparing, booking, and paying for various experiences in the city.

## Core Features

- **Service Discovery**: Browse and search for tours, events, activities, restaurants, and souvenir shops
- **Booking Management**: Complete booking flow with group booking capabilities and multi-service itineraries
- **Payment Processing**: Secure payment system with commission-based revenue model
- **Taxi Integration**: On-demand taxi service with multi-stop itinerary planning (marked as "Coming Soon")
- **Marketplace**: Restaurant reservations and souvenir shop integration
- **Loyalty Program**: Points-based rewards system with tier management
- **Reviews & Ratings**: User feedback system for service quality assurance

## Target Users

- **Tourists**: Visitors looking for local experiences and activities
- **Locals**: Residents seeking activities and events in their city
- **Service Providers**: Tour operators, event organizers, restaurants, and taxi drivers
- **Administrators**: Platform managers overseeing operations and quality

## Revenue Model

- Service providers: 10% initial commission, scaling to 18% based on performance
- Taxi services: 2% base commission with additional fees for priority and long-distance rides
- Premium features and priority services generate additional revenue streams

## Platform Strategy

Multi-platform approach with Kotlin Multiplatform Mobile for native mobile apps and Next.js for responsive web platform. Phased rollout strategy allows for incremental feature deployment based on regulatory approval and market readiness.
